Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Tell5 ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our WhatsApp business platform.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and business details. If you sign in with Google, we collect your Google account email and profile information.

WhatsApp Messages: We process message content, sender phone numbers, and message timestamps to provide AI-powered replies and store conversation history.

Business Data: Business profiles, product listings, pricing, and customer communications you manage through the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain the Tell5 platform
  • To generate AI-powered replies to customer messages
  • To store and display conversation history in your dashboard
  • To improve our AI models and service quality
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Retention

Conversations are stored for up to 7 days, after which they are automatically deleted. You can export your data as CSV before deletion. Business profiles and account information are retained until you delete your account.

Data is stored on secured servers using industry-standard encryption (PostgreSQL with SSL, MongoDB Atlas with TLS).

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Twilio — WhatsApp message sending/receiving (when using Twilio integration)
  • MongoDB Atlas — Conversation and profile storage
  • Google AI / Groq / OpenRouter — AI model providers for message classification and reply generation
  • Sentry — Error monitoring and crash reporting

Each service processes data under their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with third-party service providers only as necessary to operate the platform, and only under contractual obligations to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your conversation data as CSV
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Disable the AI pipeline at any time from Settings

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security audits.
